% ── Abstract ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\begin{abstract}
We present the first application of Grassmann manifold sequence mixing
to automatic speech recognition (ASR).  Building on the Causal Grassmann
Layer of~\citet{dong2025attention}, we replace all self-attention in a
Conformer encoder with \plucker{}-coordinate mixing on the Grassmannian
$\Gr(2,r)$ and train end-to-end with connectionist temporal
classification (CTC).  The resulting architecture is \emph{entirely
attention-free}: sequence-level interaction is performed by embedding
pairs of reduced hidden states as two-dimensional subspaces, encoding
their relative geometry via \plucker{} coordinates, and fusing the
result through a learned sigmoid gate.  Local phonetic structure is
captured by depthwise separable convolutions in a Macaron-style
Conformer sandwich.

On LibriSpeech \texttt{train-clean-100}, an 11\,M-parameter model
achieves 5.6\% character error rate (CER) with greedy CTC decoding
after 50~epochs of training on a single T4~GPU (\(\sim\)\$4 compute).
At inference the model transcribes 18 seconds of audio in 195\,ms on a
MacBook Air CPU---92$\times$ real-time---without quantisation or
hardware-specific optimisation.  A controlled comparison against a
size-matched attention-based Conformer on the same data shows the
Grassmann encoder trailing by approximately 2 CER points at epoch~16 of
70, with the gap closing at each checkpoint.

We further report ablations isolating the contribution of the Grassmann
geometry from the multiplicative mixing mechanism, and provide a
mean-first reformulation of the \plucker{} mixer that yields a
3.5$\times$ wall-clock speedup with mathematically identical output.
All code, trained ONNX models, and training logs are publicly available.
\end{abstract}
